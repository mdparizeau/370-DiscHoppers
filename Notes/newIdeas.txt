Level Ideas
1.) A dynamic, non-linear level in which the player would have to traverse from
the start zone to the end zone, at which point, the the end zone would become the 
new start zone, and the end zone would move to a new location, effectly operating
as checkpoints. This would encourage players to look ahead to the next checkpoint 
while challenging them to maintain momentum while making tight turns and finding 
creative pathing.

Obstacle Ideas
1.) Glass barrier that operates as a speed check: onHit (will swithc to onOverlap
if hit causes player to lose momentum), checks player speed. If too low, player
runs into the glass like a bird into a window. If fast enough, player will break 
through the glass and the glass will procedurely shatter into pieces (there is a 
way to do this in UE so that it's different every time).
2.) Wall of moveable blocks with different colors according to their weights. Walls
will create a kind of target in which the lightest blocks are toward the middle 
and the heaviest are toward the perimeter, incentivizing players' percision by 
allowing them greater momentum preservation.
3.) Wall that launches character opposite of their velocity when hitting wall 
according to the wall's normal. Would be helpful for 180s and U-turns, especially 
for the dynamic, non-linear levels.
